Project Overview & Goals

VibeCraft 254 was built to address the booming home entertainment and cozy projector setup culture in Kenya. The goal was to establish a trustworthy, visually striking online boutique that makes high-ticket projector discovery, spec comparison, and checkout seamless.

Using a minimalist dark aesthetic with warm neon accents, the storefront highlights crisp 4K projector capabilities, sound bars, and projection screens with rich product cards, real-time stock indicators, and customer reviews.
